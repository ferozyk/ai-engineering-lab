# LinkedIn Content Standards

## Goal

Present the author as an experienced backend engineer who engineers reliable AI systems, through technical understanding, practical implementation, engineering judgement, measured results and lessons learned. Avoid hype and marketing language.

## Post structure

1. Opening: a technical question or engineering challenge
2. Context: why the problem matters in real systems
3. What I Built: the competing implementations
4. What I Measured: the actual metrics and methodology
5. What I Discovered: measured results and trade-offs
6. Key Takeaway: when each approach makes sense
7. Closing: an invitation to technical discussion
8. Hashtags: a small number of relevant technical tags

## Tone

- Professional
- Technically credible
- Conversational
- Clear
- Not overly promotional
- Suitable for a senior software engineering audience

## Versions

Prepare two versions when useful: a concise post and a more detailed technical post.

## Integrity rules

- Make no claim beyond what the results support.
- Never invent personal experiences or claim work that was not done.
- Link to the experiment on GitHub.

## Visual assets

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Landscape | 1200 x 627 px |
| Portrait | 1080 x 1350 px |

Content: experiment title, compared approaches, measured findings, one takeaway, repository link. No decorative AI imagery. Assets are generated from benchmark data.

## Publication

Nothing is published automatically. All drafts are prepared for the author's review.
